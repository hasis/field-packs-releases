# theme_89mph

A look for [themes](../themes/README.md): **89MPH**, a car head unit. Part of **tape views 1**. Version 5. Needs themes (alone it loads and does nothing).

- **Tape screen:** a header of segment readouts (the section's name in big letters, bar.beat, the time M:SS:FF), lamps
  (PLAY, LOOP, CUE), the tempo and the selected track; a status line with where the tape goes next (`CUE CHORUS 21.1
  IN 3.2`, `LOOP ...`); dot-matrix lanes, the selected track as a framed plate, muted tracks dashed.
- **PERFORM face:** the parts as a list of the head unit's rows, the playing one framed with its bar cells filling, the
  cued one dashed; few parts, bigger rows; the punch-in strip at the bottom.
- **Mixer:** TE's levels page as strips: a 12-segment meter with a held peak and a PEAK lamp, the pan as dots on an arc,
  the volume in segment digits, MUTE.

Nothing is told by colour alone: filled / framed / dashed, lit lamps against rings, solid against hollow triangles.
